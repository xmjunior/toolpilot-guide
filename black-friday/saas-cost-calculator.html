Free Black Friday buyer checklist

  • Confirm the deal on the vendor's own site.
  • Check whether the discount applies only to new customers, annual billing or the first term.
  • Record the renewal price before checkout.
  • Add API usage, hosting, storage, email delivery and required add-ons.
  • Estimate maintenance and migration time.
  • Check cancellation, refund, export and data ownership terms.
  • Only buy if the product solves a current business problem.
