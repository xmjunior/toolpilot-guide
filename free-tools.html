01 · Test a renewal increase

My current annual software cost is [amount]. Renewal changes the price to [amount]. Required features: [list]. Calculate the difference, compare the cost of staying versus switching, and identify one reversible test. Mark missing assumptions. Verify against invoices.

02 · Audit a Black Friday offer

Use this offer and its terms: [redacted text]. Extract promotional price, renewal price/date, eligible tier, seat or usage limits, refund terms, and exclusions. Quote the source for each item; say unknown for missing terms. Calculate first-year and renewal-year totals only when inputs support them.

03 · Build a tool trial scorecard

Design a 30-minute test for [workflow] in [software]. Use fictional inputs, five objective tasks, pass/fail criteria and a weighted score based on [requirements]. Include export and accessibility checks. Do not report the test as completed.

04 · Clean a spreadsheet without losing data

For this redacted sample [columns and rows], propose deterministic rules to normalize dates, currencies, duplicate records and missing values. Preserve a source ID, show a before/after example and flag ambiguous rows. Never overwrite the original file without approval.

05 · Check an AI-generated claim

Extract factual claims from [text]. For each, identify what primary evidence would support it, whether the provided evidence is sufficient, and the correction if unsupported. Separate fact, interpretation and hypothesis. Do not fabricate sources or browse if unavailable.

06 · Design a reusable client intake

Turn [service] into a short intake form with only necessary fields. Separate required inputs, optional preferences, sensitive data to avoid, validation, and the human decision that must precede delivery. Provide one fictional example.

07 · Prepare a weekly work review

Using [redacted task log], organize completed outputs, measured results, unresolved blockers and the three next actions ranked by impact and effort. Do not count planned work as done. Cite the relevant log entries.

08 · Convert a tutorial into an implementation checklist

From [tutorial or documentation], extract prerequisites, ordered steps, commands to review, verification checks and rollback instructions. Mark version-specific or unsupported steps. Do not execute commands or assume access to accounts.

FREE-FIRST BUYING GUIDE · NOVEMBER 27, 2026

Make better videos before buying another subscription.

Start with free software if it meets your needs. Consider paid automation only after you measure how much editing time it actually saves.

Record your screen for free

OBS Studio is open-source recording and streaming software. It can be used commercially without a subscription or watermark. It does not automatically turn long recordings into edited Shorts.

Official OBS documentation ↗

Edit and caption locally

Kdenlive is a free open-source video editor. Its speech-to-text tools can create subtitles using configured Vosk or Whisper models. Installation, model setup, processing time and manual corrections still matter.

Official Kdenlive caption guide ↗

Compare automated editing

reap advertises clipping, captions and agent integrations. Its own site lists a free plan and an annually billed Creator plan. AppSumo also lists lifetime tiers with monthly usage limits. These are vendor-described features, not our own hands-on results.

Official pricing ↗

Illustrative decision: if a tool costs $80.10 once versus $9.99 per month, the simple price-only crossover is just over eight months. If the monthly alternative is actually billed as an annual $119.88 payment, compare the real cash paid at checkout instead. The plans may have different limits or features; include export quality, processing, credits, refunds, migration and time. A free tool may be the better choice.
