HOW TO CHOOSE

Start with the workflow, then compare the price.

Email tools look similar until you map the job they must do. A newsletter-only business, a sales team with lead scoring and a transactional ecommerce operation should not buy the same stack.

1. Sending model

Check contact limits, monthly send limits and whether the free tier is useful beyond a short trial.

2. Automation depth

List the actual automations you need: welcome sequence, lead scoring, abandoned cart, branching logic, CRM handoff or transactional email.

3. Real operating cost

Model the next contact tier, add-ons, extra seats and staff time. Use the same assumptions for every provider.
