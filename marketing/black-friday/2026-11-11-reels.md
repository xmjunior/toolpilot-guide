## Reel 1 — The 70% discount trap
**Audience:** US/UK/EU SaaS buyers
**Hook (0-3s):** “A 70% Black Friday discount can still cost you more next year.”
**Script (30-40s):**
Black Friday SaaS deals look simple: take the normal price, subtract the discount, buy.
But that misses the number that matters: renewal cost.
Before checkout, write down four things: discounted term, renewal price, required add-ons, and the cost of your time to maintain it.
A cheap tool with expensive APIs or server work can become the expensive option.

## Reel 2 — Free software is not always free
**Hook (0-3s):** “Self-hosted software can cost $0 and still be the expensive choice.”
**Script (30-45s):**
A free software licence is only one line in the budget.
Add hosting, email delivery, backups, monitoring, updates, security and the hours you spend fixing it.
Then compare that total with a managed platform.
Sometimes self-hosting wins. Sometimes paying a subscription is cheaper.
The decision should come from your actual workload, not the word “free.”
