EDITORIAL STANDARDS

How ToolPilot Guide researches software

Our goal is simple: help readers compare software with current, checkable information before they pay. We do not sell rankings and we do not promise financial results.

Last updated November 24, 2026.

1. Primary sources first

When we state a current price, plan limit, feature or affiliate condition, we prefer the vendor's own pricing page, help center, documentation, terms or official announcement. Vendor information can change, so the provider's live pages remain the final source of truth.

2. One decision, comparable criteria

Comparisons focus on the decision a buyer is actually making. Depending on the category, we compare current price, free-plan limits, billing structure, renewal risk, export options, operating costs, monetization rules, integrations and who each option is best suited for.

3. We separate facts from assumptions

Verified product facts are presented as facts. Worked examples, break-even calculations and scenarios are labelled as examples or assumptions. Calculators are decision-support tools, not forecasts or income promises.

4. Affiliate relationships do not decide the verdict

Some links are affiliate or partner links and are labelled as such. ToolPilot Guide may earn a commission from a qualifying purchase. A provider can still be ranked lower, excluded or described as a poor fit when the evidence points that way. When no affiliate relationship exists, we can still link to the official provider.

5. We show dates and encourage re-checking

Software pricing and plan rules change quickly. Important comparisons show when key information was checked. Before buying, readers should verify the current price, eligibility, taxes, renewal terms and final checkout directly with the provider.

6. Free tools stay usable without purchase

Our calculators and decision tools are designed to work without requiring a software purchase. Optional analytics and email signups are described separately in our privacy policy.

7. What we do not do

  • No paid ranking disguised as editorial judgment.
  • No fake reviews, fake testimonials or fake scarcity.
  • No guaranteed-income claims.
  • No copying vendor marketing claims without context.
  • No buying backlinks or publishing mass-generated pages simply to increase page count.

8. Corrections and updates

If a vendor changes pricing or terms, the relevant guide should be updated rather than silently preserving outdated claims. Older seasonal content may be retained for reference, repositioned or removed when it no longer helps a current buying decision.
