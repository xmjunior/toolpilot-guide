What ToolPilot Guide does

ToolPilot Guide is an educational comparison website with optional affiliate referrals. We do not operate a checkout. Some free tools offer an optional email signup for deal and buying-guide updates.

Email watchlist

If you voluntarily join a ToolPilot watchlist, we store your email address, consent status, signup source, locale, optional referrer and signup timestamp in our database. We use it only for the ToolPilot updates described next to the form. You can use our free calculators without joining.

Conversion measurement

We record limited first-party anonymous events such as page views, clicks on disclosed affiliate links and successful watchlist submissions. These application records contain event type, page path, a coarse source label, target label where relevant and timestamp. They do not contain the email address, cookies, advertising IDs or a fingerprint. Our infrastructure providers may separately process standard network/security logs such as IP address and user agent for delivery, abuse prevention and security.

Optional site analytics

We use Metricool only when you explicitly choose “Accept analytics” on the consent banner. Its tracker may process device identifiers, browsing interactions and technical information under its own privacy terms. If you decline, the Metricool tracking script is not loaded. Consent is recorded in your browser's local storage.

Affiliate referrals

Some links are disclosed partner links to beehiiv. When you click an affiliate link, the destination may set cookies or collect data under the destination website's policies. We may earn a commission on qualifying paid subscriptions; we do not receive details of every visitor's browsing outside our site.

Hosting

Our hosting and database/edge-function providers may process technical request logs for delivery, performance, abuse prevention and security independently from optional Metricool analytics.

Change your consent

Click the button below to open your analytics choice again. You can also clear local storage in your browser to reset it. Declining will stop future Metricool tracking on this website, although previously collected data cannot be recalled through this control.

Questions

If you need clarification, please contact us through an official ToolPilot channel when available. Do not enter sensitive information into third-party websites without reviewing their policies.
