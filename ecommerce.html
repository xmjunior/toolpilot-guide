HOW TO CHOOSE

Model the store you actually plan to run.

Platform price is only one line in the budget. A hosted platform can cost more in subscriptions but save technical time; a self-hosted stack can start cheaper and become more expensive through hosting, extensions and maintenance.

1. Platform model

Decide whether you want a hosted system with bundled infrastructure or an open-source stack you control.

2. Operating cost

Add apps, extensions, hosting, payment fees, backups, development and staff time.

3. Switching risk

Check data export, theme/app dependencies, migration work and how hard it would be to change later.

QuestionShopify-style hostedWooCommerce-style self-hosted
Who manages infrastructure?Mostly the platformYou or your hosting/provider stack
Cost visibilityMore predictable subscription baseWider range depending on hosting/extensions
Technical flexibilityControlled platform ecosystemHigher control and customization
Maintenance burdenLower infrastructure burdenHigher responsibility for updates and compatibility
