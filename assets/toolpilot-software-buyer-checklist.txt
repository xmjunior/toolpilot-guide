ToolPilot Guide — Free Software Buyer Checklist
https://toolpilot-guide.onrender.com/free-tools.html
Use this checklist before purchasing or renewing any business software.

[ ] Define the job and the measurable result the tool must deliver.
[ ] Check the free plan, usage limits and at least two alternatives.
[ ] Calculate total first-year cost, including seats, add-ons, taxes and setup.
[ ] Check renewal price, billing period, cancellation and refund conditions.
[ ] Identify duplicate subscriptions and unused seats.
[ ] Verify data export, integration and migration costs.
[ ] Test the core workflow with non-sensitive sample data.
[ ] Set a calendar reminder before trial or renewal.
[ ] Decide: buy, trial, keep, downgrade or cancel.

Budget tracker columns:
Tool | Purpose | Seats | Monthly cost | Annual cost | Renewal date | Last used | Decision
Compare totals for the same currency and billing period. Do not assume promotional prices recur.
