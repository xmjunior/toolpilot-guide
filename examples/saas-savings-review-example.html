What a SaaS Savings Review can look like.

This example uses fictional numbers to show the type of analysis ToolPilot can provide. It is not a testimonial and does not claim real savings.

Example company

A 10-person agency pays for 12 software tools at an estimated $1,240/month. Several tools overlap in project management, forms and email automation.

FindingWhy it mattersNext question
Two project-management toolsPossible duplicate subscription and onboarding overheadWhich workflows genuinely require both?
Email platform at low utilizationPaid tier may exceed current useWould a lower tier or migration cover the same workflow?
Three form/landing-page toolsFeatures may overlap with the CRM/email stackCould one platform replace two subscriptions?
Annual prepayment approaching renewalSwitching decision has a deadlineWhat is the migration cost before renewal?

Example review conclusion

The review would not tell the company to cancel tools blindly. It would rank the next three decisions: first confirm whether both project-management tools are necessary; second model the email platform at the next lower tier; third calculate migration cost before the annual renewal.

What we would not do

  • No guaranteed savings claim.
  • No request for passwords or customer data.
  • No recommendation based only on affiliate commission.
  • No cancellation advice without checking workflow and migration risk.
