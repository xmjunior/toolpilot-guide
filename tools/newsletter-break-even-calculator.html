Newsletter break-even calculator

How many subscribers would you need to cover monthly newsletter costs? Explore scenarios without sharing your data or creating an account.

Hypothetical monthly net result

$150

Formula: subscribers × hypothetical purchase rate × margin per sale − fixed monthly cost. This is an illustrative scenario, not a revenue forecast. It assumes one sale per converting subscriber in the month, without refunds or taxes.

How should you use the results?

Start with a conservative purchase-rate estimate. Add all known costs, including newsletter software, content work, acquisition and tools. Use the calculator to compare scenarios, not to assume readers will buy.
