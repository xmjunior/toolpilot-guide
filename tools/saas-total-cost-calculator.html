What this catches that pricing pages often hide

  • Per-seat multiplication.
  • Add-ons and usage-based charges.
  • Migration and setup work.
  • The ongoing human time required to maintain the tool.
  • The difference between a discounted annual contract and the real operating cost.

Use the result

Compare two or three tools using the same assumptions. If the cheaper option requires substantially more staff time, it may not be cheaper in practice.

Transparent assumption:

This calculator does not know your vendor contract, taxes, exchange rates or future price changes. It is a planning model, not a quote.
