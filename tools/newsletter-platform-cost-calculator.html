What is included and excluded?

This compares platform subscriptions and revenue shares only. It excludes Stripe and other processors, sales taxes, currency conversion, refunds, acquisition and production. The remainder is not net profit. It does not assume identical payment-processing fees.

The scenario assumes monthly subscriptions without churn or discounts. Normalize annual subscriptions to the same period. Annual beehiiv billing requires the full annual payment; dividing by 12 does not turn it into a monthly contract.

Transparent formula

Revenue = paying subscribers × monthly price. Platform cost = fixed fee + revenue × commission rate. With 100 people paying $5, a $49 fee is $1 less than 10% of $500. Other expenses are excluded.
