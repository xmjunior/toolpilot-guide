Which plan should a small business compare first?

Basic is the natural baseline for most new stores. Move beyond it only when the additional plan features or transaction economics solve a real business constraint. Do not select a higher tier only because the store might grow later.

Costs outside the plan

Apps, premium themes, third-party services, staff time and payment-provider choices can materially change the total operating cost. A store using several paid apps can spend far more than the headline plan price suggests.

Annual billing trade-off

Annual billing lowers the monthly-equivalent plan cost, but it increases commitment. Before prepaying, confirm that the platform fits the workflow, required integrations and expected store volume.

Questions before upgrading

  • Which higher-tier feature creates measurable value?
  • How many paid apps will the store need?
  • Will additional staff accounts or integrations affect cost?
  • What is the one-year total including setup and maintenance?

Affiliate status
