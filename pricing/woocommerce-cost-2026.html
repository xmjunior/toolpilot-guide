WOOCOMMERCE COST · 2026

WooCommerce is free. Running a store is not.

Reviewed November 24, 2026 from WooCommerce official pricing.

Official cost anchors

  • WooCommerce core platform: free.
  • Hosting: WooCommerce says $25–$350/month for most stores.
  • Extensions: WooCommerce cites $29–$299/year per extension.
  • Payment processor fees: depend on the processor.

Why total cost varies so much

Hosting performance, premium extensions, development support, backups, security and maintenance can create a much wider cost range than a hosted platform.
