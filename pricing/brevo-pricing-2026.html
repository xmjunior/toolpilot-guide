Brevo pricing: what do you actually get?

Use the smallest plan that solves today's job. Model the next contact tier before annual billing.

Reviewed November 24, 2026.

Free or trial

Free · 300 email sends/day · up to 100,000 stored contacts

Paid starting point

Starter from $9/month · Standard from $18/month

Best fit

Low-cost multichannel email, transactional messaging and automation with a generous free entry point.

What to watch

Daily send limits and tier-specific automation/features matter more than raw contact storage.

Who should compare Brevo seriously?

Brevo is worth investigating when you need email marketing and transactional messaging in one lower-cost stack, especially if daily sending limits fit your volume. It can be attractive for small businesses that care more about practical sending and automation than creator-specific publishing features.

Questions before upgrading

  • Will the daily send limit block campaigns at your real volume?
  • Do you need transactional email as well as marketing campaigns?
  • Which automation features are available on the tier you actually need?
  • Will contact storage, seats or advanced features change the total cost?
  • Are you comparing annual cost with equivalent limits on other platforms?

Real-cost check

Model sends, users, automation needs and any add-ons together. A low headline plan can become expensive if you need higher sending capacity or premium features early.

Affiliate status

Brevo has a PartnerStack affiliate program, but approval requires an established relevant website and professional email. ToolPilot has no active affiliate link yet.
