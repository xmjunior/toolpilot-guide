Kit pricing: what will you actually pay?

Start with the smallest plan that solves today's problem. Model the next subscriber tier before committing to annual billing.

Free plan

$0 · up to 10,000 subscribers · unlimited broadcasts

First meaningful paid step

Creator: $33/mo equivalent billed yearly ($390/year) or about $39 month-to-month at 1,000 subscribers

Who should pay?

Best when email sells courses, coaching, digital products or services through automations.

What can make the price misleading?

Free lacks the paid automations/sequences stack; advanced creator features require Creator or Pro. Also include add-ons, payment processing, team seats, API usage, migration and the price at your next list size.

Upgrade rule

Upgrade only when a paid feature is tied to a measurable outcome you already need.
