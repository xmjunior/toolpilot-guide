beehiiv pricing: what will you actually pay?

beehiiv can start at $0, but the paid plans change with subscriber count. Check the next list-size tier before committing to annual billing.

Quick answer

Free: $0, with beehiiv currently supporting up to 2,500 active subscribers and unlimited sends.

Lite: starts at $49/month equivalent when billed yearly at the 1,000-subscriber tier. Monthly billing starts at $59/month.

Pro: starts at $95/month equivalent when billed yearly at the 1,000-subscriber tier. Monthly billing starts at $109/month.

Pricing rises as your active subscriber count grows, so a platform that looks inexpensive at 1,000 subscribers may cost materially more at 10,000 or 50,000.

When does Lite make sense?

Lite is the first meaningful paid step if you need paid subscriptions, automations, ad monetization, A/B testing or other growth features that are not part of the Free plan. Do not upgrade only because your newsletter is growing; upgrade when a paid feature is tied to a measurable outcome.

When does Pro make sense?

Pro is aimed at publishers that need features such as advanced analytics, webhooks, dynamic content, higher AI allowances or priority support. If you do not actively use those capabilities, compare the incremental cost with Lite before upgrading.

The cost most buyers miss

The headline plan price is only one part of total cost. Model the price at your expected subscriber count 6–12 months from now and include payment-processing fees, migration time, add-ons and any adjacent tools you may still need.
