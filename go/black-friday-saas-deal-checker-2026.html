What a good Black Friday software deal must survive

  • Renewal test: what will you pay when the headline discount disappears?
  • Usage test: are the paid features solving a current problem or a hypothetical future one?
  • Hidden-cost test: APIs, server, email delivery, storage, add-ons and maintenance count.
  • Exit test: how hard is cancellation, export and migration?
  • Free-alternative test: can a free plan cover the next 90 days?
