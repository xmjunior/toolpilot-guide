The real cost of a newsletter platform: 7 checks before buying

A business decision guide for independent publishers, entrepreneurs and small teams. Updated November 8, 2026.

Editorial disclosure: ToolPilot Guide may receive commissions from qualifying beehiiv subscriptions through links on this page. We currently have no verified sales or payouts. This guide does not recommend purchasing a plan before your needs justify it.

1. Cost per active reader, not just the sticker price

Compare the total monthly bill against the number of engaged subscribers, not merely the mailing-list size. Estimate your expected list size in three scenarios: 100, 1,000 and 10,000 people. Recheck current vendor pricing and sending limits for each scenario.

2. Export and switching costs

Confirm that you can export email addresses and permitted subscriber data, retrieve past issues and redirect your audience if you leave. The ability to leave a platform without losing your audience is part of the economic value.

3. Deliverability basics

Look for domain authentication, clear sending policies, suppression management and unsubscribe handling. Deliverability depends on your list quality and sending practices as well as software.

4. Editorial workflow

Check the editor, scheduling options, team roles and reusable templates. A low subscription price can be expensive if publishing takes too much time.

5. Monetization eligibility

Do not assume all advertisement, subscription or referral features are available to every account or country. Check eligibility, fee schedules, payment methods and tax requirements before relying on them for revenue.

6. Track the outcome that matters

Measure subscriber acquisition cost, verified opt-ins, engagement and recurring revenue. A large list with weak engagement may be less useful than a smaller one with genuinely interested readers.

7. Free trial and renewal

Verify what happens when a free trial ends, which features are removed at downgrade, renewal pricing, and how cancellation works. Save links to the official terms in your notes.
