The hidden cost test

A free plan can still cost time. Before choosing, score each platform on migration effort, custom domain needs, automation, forms, analytics, export, payment processing, integrations and the hours you will spend working around missing features.

Rule: if the free plan handles the next 90 days, use it. If a paid feature saves more time or money than it costs, then upgrade. Do not prepay a long term just because the discount percentage is large.

Which one should you test first?

beehiiv Free: test if you want a publication-first workflow and expect to evaluate paid subscriptions, advertising or automations later.

Sender Free Forever: test if your priority is email marketing, forms, landing pages and automation within the stated monthly email allowance. Sender currently also advertises a Black Friday 2026 promotion on paid plans; verify checkout eligibility before committing.

Substack: test if you want to avoid a fixed publishing subscription and are comfortable with a revenue-share model when you charge readers.
