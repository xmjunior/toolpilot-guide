1. Who should consider beehiiv?

Start with beehiiv if the core product you intend to build is an ongoing email publication. You need a clear editorial calendar, a consistent publishing process and a plan for getting readers. The software alone does not create an audience.

2. The buying checklist

  • Audience size: Check subscriber and sending limits for the plan you expect to use.
  • Publishing workflow: Make sure the editor, scheduling and website functions fit your workflow.
  • Data portability: Confirm subscriber export options and what happens if you leave.
  • Monetization: Check which monetization features require paid tiers and local eligibility.
  • Recurring cost: Compare current prices, renewal conditions and additional charges directly with the vendor.

4. Questions before you subscribe

Can you start without paying?

Review the current free plan and trial terms on the official beehiiv website. Offers, eligibility and limits can change.

Will a newsletter make money automatically?

No. A successful publication needs real readers, repeat engagement and a viable product or monetization path. There is no guaranteed income from using a publishing tool.

What should you measure?

Track legitimate signups, reader retention, publishing consistency and revenue attributable to the publication. Avoid measuring only pageviews or superficial vanity metrics.

5. Next step

Write a one-sentence promise for your newsletter and plan four useful editions before selecting a paid tier. Then compare features and current costs with your actual workflow.
