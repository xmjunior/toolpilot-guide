How to choose without overpaying

Starting with no paid subscribers? Test a free managed option first if it supports your required workflow; don't pay for infrastructure you cannot yet justify. Have paying subscribers? Compare fixed subscriptions against revenue share and check payment processing separately. Have technical staff? Self-hosting deserves a trial only after you estimate incident response, upgrades, security and deliverability.

Recommendation: choose the lowest sustainable total cost that meets your actual needs, not the platform with the highest referral payout. For a publication-first workflow, beehiiv is one managed option worth testing; for a no-fixed-publishing-fee model, compare Substack. Neither is automatically best.
