Shopify vs WooCommerce: compare the cost behind the monthly price.

A $0 core plugin can still cost more after hosting, extensions and maintenance. A hosted platform can still cost more after apps and transaction choices.

Cost buckets to model

  • Monthly/annual platform subscription.
  • Hosting and backups.
  • Paid apps/extensions.
  • Payment and third-party provider fees.
  • Developer or agency time.
  • Migration and setup.
  • Ongoing admin/maintenance hours.

Current official anchors

Shopify Basic is currently $39/month on monthly billing or $29/month billed yearly. WooCommerce core is free, while WooCommerce itself estimates hosting at $25–$350/month for most stores and extensions at $29–$299/year each.
