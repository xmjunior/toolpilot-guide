Shopify vs WooCommerce: which costs less for your business?

Shopify bundles hosting and platform operations. WooCommerce has no core platform fee but makes you assemble hosting and extensions.

Reviewed November 24, 2026 from Shopify and WooCommerce official pricing.

DecisionShopifyWooCommerce
Core/platformBasic $39/mo monthly or $29/mo billed yearlyCore plugin: free
HostingIncludedWooCommerce estimates $25–$350/mo for most stores
ExtensionsApp costs varyWooCommerce cites $29–$299/year per extension
Maintenance modelHosted platformWordPress + hosting + plugins managed by you/provider

Choose Shopify when…

You value predictable hosting, one platform, lower maintenance overhead and a straightforward path from trial to paid store.

Choose WooCommerce when…

You already use WordPress, want open-source control and are comfortable managing hosting, extensions and technical maintenance.
